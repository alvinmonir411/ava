## 1. How to Log In

1. Open your web browser and go to your website address followed by `/admin` (e.g. `https://yourdomain.com/admin` or `http://localhost:3000/admin`).
2. If you are not signed in, you will be directed to the secure login screen (`/admin/login`).
3. Enter your **Chambers Security PIN / Password** (Default: `13663`).
4. Click **"Access Chambers Dashboard"**.
5. You will arrive at the main administrative control center.

## 2. Navigating Your Dashboard

The top navigation bar provides one-click access to all management modules:

* **Dashboard Overview (`/admin`)**: Summary of client inquiries, active practice areas, articles, and quick shortcuts.
* **Website Settings & Content (`/admin/settings`)**: Master control center for homepage headlines, lawyer bio, past work experience, section visibility, photos, and firm contact info.
* **Practice Areas (`/admin/practices`)**: Manage all 12+ legal practice areas, service checklists, and court scopes.
* **Articles (`/admin/articles`)**: Publish and update legal insights and articles.
* **Client Reviews (`/admin/testimonials`)**: Manage featured client commendations and star ratings.
* **Inquiries (`/admin/inquiries`)**: View and manage consultation requests submitted through the contact form.

## 3. Homepage Section Toggles (Show or Hide Sections)

You have full control over which sections are visible on your homepage:

1. Click on **Settings** in the top navigation (`/admin/settings`).
2. Select the **"Homepage Sections"** tab.
3. You will see switch toggles for optional sections:
   * **Portraits & Leadership Gallery**: Displays the portrait showcase cards.
   * **Recognition & Commendations**: Displays the "Best Law Firms in Kuala Lumpur" feature block.
   * **Chambers Overview & Quick Facts**: Displays quick credentials summary.
   * **Latest Legal Articles**: Displays your latest published insights on the homepage.
4. Click any switch to toggle it **Active (Green)** or **Hidden (Grey)**.
5. Click the purple **"Save All Settings & Content"** button at the top right to make your changes live immediately.

## 7. Managing Practice Areas & Legal Services

To edit the legal services your chambers provides:

1. Click on **Practices** (`/admin/practices`) in the top navigation.
2. You will see all 12 practice areas listed (such as *Company Matters & Commercial Agreements*, *Dispute Resolution & Litigation*, *Property Conveyancing*, *Family & Divorce*, etc.).
3. Click **"Edit Practice Area"** on any card to update:
   * Practice Title & Category
   * Short Summary (displayed on cards)
   * Full Detailed Description
   * Key Malaysian Statutes cited (e.g. Companies Act 2016, Law Reform (Marriage & Divorce) Act 1976)
   * Step-by-step Engagement Stages & Checklists
   * Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
4. Click **"Save Practice Area"** to update the live service page.

## 8. Publishing Legal Articles & Client Guides

To publish legal commentary, client guidance, or statutory updates:

1. Click on **Articles** (`/admin/articles`) in the top navigation.
2. To create a new article: Click **"Write New Article"** at the top right.
3. Fill in:
   * **Title**: e.g., *Understanding Property Conveyancing in Malaysia: Complete Legal Guide*
   * **Category**: e.g., *Property & Real Estate Law*
   * **Read Time**: e.g., *5 min read*
   * **Summary / Excerpt**: 1–2 sentences summarizing the article for Google search and preview cards.
   * **Full Article Content**: Write your analysis using paragraphs, headings, and bullet points.
4. Click **"Publish Article"**. The article is instantly live under `/articles` and on the homepage (if the Articles section toggle is enabled).

## 11. Saving and Publishing Your Changes

* Whenever you edit text, upload an image, or flip a toggle in `/admin/settings`, look at the top-right corner.
